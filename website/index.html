About Mini Games

Mini Games is an open source project that brings together a variety of fun and innovative mini games under one roof. Our goal is to provide a platform where you can explore, play, and even contribute to exciting projects. While our games are currently developed with Python and Pygame, we are actively working on web ports to let you play them directly in your browser.
